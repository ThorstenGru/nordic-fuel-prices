# Roadmap refresh, 2026-11-16 (supersedes the starting-position table of ROADMAP.md, 2026-10-02)

## Where we stand
- **Data:** per-station prices in about 40 countries. Germany: ANWB baseline + Tankerkoenig (test mode, rotating by age, readings carried 8-12 h, `de_tk_state.json`). Austria: e-Control, now ~2,000 stations (was 788; the API returns 10 per request, topped up by-address at ANWB places). Switzerland: ANWB only, no reference. Nordics: DK chain feeds, FI/SE crowd sources, ANWB fills gaps.
- **Trust:** accuracy meters for DE and AT (`de_accuracy.json`, `at_accuracy.json`); `stale_low` plausibility rule; chain-price flag for ANWB list prices (`chain_price`, note in the app); ANWB rating per country in `docs/ANWB_RATING_2026-11-16.md`.
- **Product:** route planner, My car, charger layers (SE NO DK DE NL BE IE IS FR GB), facilities proxy, survey and follow cards, cookieless counters (new: `visit`, `visit_fb`).
- **Growth:** daily Facebook post, Reels three times a week (with synthesised music), weekly insights, hourly town-comment replies.
