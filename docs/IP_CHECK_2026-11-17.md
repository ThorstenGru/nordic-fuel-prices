Read "IPO check" as intellectual property and licences. This is a technical review of what the repository and the site do, not legal advice; for the points marked LEGAL ask a lawyer or the source owner. Labels: [verified] = read in the vendor's own page today; [repo] = found in this repository; [known] = general knowledge to confirm.

## B. Maps and services the app calls from visitors' browsers
- **tile.openstreetmap.org (map tiles)** [repo + verified policy]: allowed for normal interactive viewing with visible attribution and valid Referer; but heavy use or "commercial or donation-seeking services" may be blocked without notice, no SLA; bulk/offline tile download is forbidden. **Risk: medium and growing with traffic.** Fix: OpenFreeMap (commercial use allowed, no key, attribution required, no SLA [verified]) or PMTiles on R2.
- **Nominatim (address search) and OSRM / Valhalla demo servers** [repo, CSP and texts]: public demo services with fair-use policies (Nominatim: about 1 request per second, must identify the app) [known]. The route proxy Worker already shields the route engine; address search still goes from the visitor's browser. Fix: route all geocoding through the Worker with a User-Agent that names the app and a cache.
- **OpenStreetMap data (ODbL)**: attribution and "share-alike for derived databases". We publish station locations derived from OSM for SE/AT place names (`osm=True`); the footers credit OpenStreetMap contributors [repo]. Keep the credit on every page that uses place names.
- **Overpass mirrors** (facilities near a station): public, can be at capacity (observed); we cache and proxy. Same fair-use rules.

## D. Names, brands, trademarks
- **"ANWB", "Tankerkoenig", chain names (Shell, Circle K ...)** appear as source labels and station names: nominative use to identify sources/stations, which is normally allowed; avoid logos of these brands. [repo: no logos found]
- **"EuroFuelPrices" / "Euro Fuel Prices"**: no trademark search done. A quick check in the EUIPO and Swedish PRV registers for "EuroFuelPrices" (classes 9, 35, 42) is cheap and worth doing before the Facebook push grows the name. **LEGAL.**
- **Facebook/Meta**: automated posting with the Page's own token is allowed under the Platform Terms; the bot answers comments, does not message people unprompted and stores comment ids only. Do not use Meta's logo beyond the follow link. Page-name rule (no mixed capitals) is why the Page is "Euro Fuel Prices".

## E. Privacy and legal duties
- **No cookies; cookieless counters** (`/api/poll`, D1 stores day + event + count) and Cloudflare Web Analytics [repo]. The Worker uses the IP only as a rate-limit key and stores none [repo]. **Good.**
- **localStorage** holds preferences (car profile, saved stations, language, `efp_visits`, snooze dates) [repo]. Preferences the user sets are exempt in most places; counters such as `efp_visits` that only steer a survey card are a grey area under the ePrivacy rules (DE/AT in particular). Recommendation: keep them, say so in the privacy text, and offer "clear my data".
- **Third-party calls from the browser** (OSM tiles, Nominatim, OSRM/Valhalla) transmit the visitor's IP address to those operators. The privacy text should name them (it already names Nominatim and OSRM [repo]); add the tile server.
- **No imprint / contact on the site** [repo: `about.html` has no contact]. In Germany and Austria a regular online service normally needs provider identification (Impressum); purely private non-commercial sites are exempt, but a public service with a Facebook Page and a growth plan should have one. Cheapest safe route: an About/Contact page with name, country, and an email on our own domain (Cloudflare Email Routing, free). **LEGAL.**
- **GDPR on Facebook**: the Page is visited by people; Facebook is the controller for Page analytics; we only read aggregate insights and comment text addressed to the Page. The bot's reply log stores comment ids, not names [repo].
- **Public repository content**: the docs contain third-party contact addresses (e.g. info@tankerkoenig.de) and quotes of their replies; the owner's own address does not appear [repo scan]. Consider moving the outreach drafts and quoted replies to a private place and keeping only the one-line status in `PERMISSIONS.md`.
