## A. Honest assessment of the current route planner (v1.10.0 "Preview", live at v2.0.1)

### What it does today [code]
1. Prices sheet, then Route tab (`renderRoute`). Two text boxes (From, To), a "use my location" button, a "pick on map" button, corridor width chips (2 / 3 / 5 km), and a "hide prices of unknown age" tick.
2. Places are found with Nominatim, on submit only (`rtGeocode`), with a 1.1 s pause between the two lookups to respect the 1 request per second rule.
3. The route comes from the free OSRM demo server, `router.project-osrm.org`, car profile, A to B only (`rtGo`). Ferries and tolls are not considered.
4. Which countries the route touches is worked out from country bounding boxes sampled every ~15 km; missing country files are loaded in parallel (`loadCC`).
5. Corridor search (`rtCorridor`): a grid of ~5.5 km cells over the polyline, then exact point-to-segment distance. Stations with a price for the chosen fuel (95 or diesel only) inside the corridor become candidates.
6. Ranking (`rtRank`): detour is estimated as `2 x offset x 1.3` km. Net cost = `(tank litres + detourKm x consumption/100) x EUR price`. So it always assumes you fill the whole tank. Prices older than 10 h are dropped (owner decision 2026-10-07). It keeps the best 10, makes sure each country on the route gets its own best pump first, and spaces picks at least 3 km apart.
7. Display: "x EUR/L below the route median", age and source per price, a "Go" button (nav-app chooser) and "Route via here" (Google Maps with the station as one waypoint).

### What is good
- Honest wording: "Preview", "estimate", prices labelled with source and age, no "live" claims.
- Everything except two calls (Nominatim, OSRM) runs in the browser on data already loaded. No account, no tracking.
- The My car profile already stores tank, consumption and, for plug-in hybrids, battery kWh, electric range, home electricity price and charging efficiency [code: `carSet`, `renderCarCard`]. The route planner uses only the tank and consumption, not the PHEV fields.

### Gaps, in order of how much they hurt
| # | Gap | Evidence | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Routing depends on the OSRM demo server.** Policy is non-commercial, ~1 request/s, no uptime promise. | [doc] ROUTE_PLANNER_DESIGN.md section 2; CSP in `index.html` allows only that host for routing | Can fail at any time; the UI already says "the free routing server may be busy". Not safe to promote. |
| 2 | **No ferries.** Malmö to Hamburg measured 503 km on OSRM vs 373 km on Valhalla with a ferry. | [doc] ROUTE_PLANNER_DESIGN.md | The corridor for a Baltic or Danish trip can be entirely different from what the driver will drive. The note mentions it only in a small print line. |
| 3 | **Fuel-stop model is a single "fill the whole tank" guess.** No starting fuel level, no reserve, no range check, no multi-stop plan. | [code] `rtRank` uses `R.L` (tank) as litres bought | On a long route the tool may suggest a pump 700 km along that you cannot reach. It also over-credits cheap pumps by assuming a full tank is bought there. |
| 4 | **PHEV is ignored.** Cost per km, litres needed and the value of the electric range are not used. | [code] route uses `trip.cons` and `tank` only | For the owner's own car the "net cost" is wrong: the first ~54 km are electric and cost no petrol. |
| 5 | **Detour is straight line x 1.3, never refined.** | [code] `ROAD_FACTOR = 1.3` | Across a river, motorway without exit or a border it can be badly wrong in either direction. Labelled "est." but never checked. |
| 6 | **"Saves" is relative to the route median of listed stations**, not to what the driver would otherwise pay, and the detour cost is not netted off the displayed saving. | [code] `rtCheaper`: price difference only | A pump 0.02 EUR/L cheaper but 8 km off route looks like a bargain in the card. Ranking uses net cost but the number shown does not. |
| 7 | **One fuel family only** (P95 or diesel). No P98, E85, HVO100, LPG. | [code] `rtFuelKey` | Misses the rest of the supported fuels. |
| 8 | **No via-points, no alternatives, no departure-time or "arrive with X litres".** | [code] | Cannot model "stop in Flensburg anyway". |
| 9 | **No offline or share link.** Result is lost on reload; nothing to send to a travel partner. | [code]: results held in memory (`rt.res`) only | Poor for a trip planned at home and used in the car. |
| 10 | **Typing a place is slow and fragile**: submit-only Nominatim, country-restricted (`NOM_CC`), first hit only, no disambiguation. | [code] `rtGeocode` | "Springfield"-type ambiguity; no list to choose from. Policy-clean, though. |
| 11 | **Data holes make the answer wrong in places**: Germany is on the ANWB fallback (no timestamps, mislabelled petrol slots known), Italy stale since 2026-10-16, Romania partial. | [doc] DATA_ACCURACY_2026-11-05.md, DATA_ACCURACY_2026-10-08.md | "Cheapest pump on the route" can be a stale or unverified price. The 10 h cutoff drops ANWB-only prices only if they carry a timestamp; those without one are shown as "unknown age" unless the tick is set. |
| 12 | **Privacy text is accurate but thin**: start and end go to Nominatim and OSRM; a proxy would change who sees it. | [code] `rtNote` | Must be re-written when the Worker proxy is added. |

### Verdict
A good, honest preview for a short single-country trip. Not yet trustworthy for the owner's real use (long, cross-border, PHEV) and not safe to promote because of gap 1 and gap 2.

## C. Route-planning spec (target v2: "Trip planner")

### C1. Inputs
| Input | Source | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Start, destination | text (Nominatim on submit, top-5 pick list), "my location", map tap | none |
| Via-points (later, X6) | same | none |
| Fuel | car profile, else the Prices-sheet choice | profile's fuel |
| Tank size (L), consumption (L/100 km, petrol-only) | My car (`car.tank`, `car.cons`) | profile |
| Starting fuel (L or %) | slider on the route screen | 100% for a first plan; remembered locally |
| Reserve | setting | 10% of tank, never below 5 L [assumed] |
| PHEV only: battery kWh, electric range km, electricity price per kWh, charging efficiency | My car (`car.batt`, `car.range`, `car.elec`, `car.eff`) | profile |
| PHEV only: starting battery (%) | slider | 100% if the car type is PHEV [assumed] |
| PHEV only: "charge at destination / en route" | switch | off (no charging assumed on route) |
| Corridor half-width | chips 2 / 3 / 5 km | 3 km |

Missing profile fields: the planner asks inline once ("Add consumption to get a plan") and falls back to today's behaviour, labelled "without your car".

### C2. Algorithm
1. **Route**: one call through the Worker; get polyline, distance, duration, and flags for ferries and tolls. Keep the provider name and flags in the result.
2. **Countries**: as today (bounding boxes every ~15 km). Load country files progressively, first the start country, so the first answer appears early [doc design].
3. **Corridor**: existing `rtCorridor` (grid plus point-to-segment) gives each candidate `along` (km from start) and `off` (km from route).
4. **Energy model along the route** (new). Walk the route from km 0:
   - Petrol-only car: litres burned between km a and b = `(b - a) x cons / 100`.
   - PHEV: the car first uses the battery. Electric km available at start = `electricRange x startBattery% x realWorldFactor`, where `realWorldFactor` is a profile setting (default 0.75 on motorway, since WLTP range is optimistic [assumed]). While battery lasts: electric cost per km = `kWh/100km / 100 x elecPrice / efficiency`. After that: petrol per km as above (charge-sustaining consumption, which is higher than the WLTP weighted figure; the car profile `cons` must be the petrol-only figure, and the UI must say so).
5. **Detour cost of a candidate** (replaces `2 x off x 1.3` as the quick estimate, keeps it as the fallback):
   - `detourKm = 2 x off x 1.3` (quick), later refined for the top ~10 by real routing (X2).
   - `detourEnergyCost` = detourKm priced at the car's cost per km **at that point of the route** (electric if battery remains there, else petrol at that station's price).
6. **Price saving of a candidate**: `saving = (referencePrice - price) x litresBought - detourEnergyCost`, where
   - `referencePrice` = median of dated corridor prices near this stretch (within +/-100 km along), in EUR; not the whole-route median [change from today],
   - `litresBought` = litres actually needed for the next leg (see 7), not a full tank.
   - Currency: price in EUR via the already-loaded stats (`rtRate`); show local currency next to it.
7. **Stop planning** (X1; N5 is the cut-down version):
   - Usable range = `(fuelNow - reserve) / consPetrol x 100` km of petrol driving, plus electric km for PHEV.
   - If the destination is reachable with reserve intact: answer "No stop needed", plus the best optional pump with its net saving (only if saving > a small threshold, e.g. 1 EUR [assumed]).
   - Else: greedy lookahead on candidates sorted by `along`: buy just enough to reach the next candidate that is cheaper and within range; if none, fill to a sensible level. DP over fuel states (5 L steps) is the later upgrade [doc design section 3].
8. **Ranking and trust rules**: net saving first; then fresher price; then source grade. Candidates with `unverified`, `octane_order` or "stale country" flags are shown below the line, never as "best". Prices older than the staleness cutoff (today 10 h) are not offered; unknown age is labelled and, by default, ranked below dated prices.
9. **Output**: one recommended plan (0 to 3 stops) and a list of the 10 cheapest pumps by net cost; each row: price, local currency, age chip, source, detour km (with "est." or "checked"), net saving "about X EUR".

### C3. UI flow (Nordic Paper design)
Existing tokens and components are reused (bottom sheet, `tcard`, chips, `rt-stop` rows); no new visual language.
1. **Route tab, collapsed state**: From, To, one "Plan" button. "My car" summary chip under the fields ("Santa Fe PHEV, petrol 95, battery 100%") that opens the car profile.
2. **Fuel and range strip** (one line, tappable): starting fuel %, reserve, and, for a PHEV, battery %. Defaults are shown, not hidden.
3. **Progress line** while loading: "Route found (via ferry), loading Germany...". Result appears as soon as the first country is ready and updates when others arrive.
4. **Result sheet** (half height): headline sentence ("No fuel stop needed. Best optional stop saves about 2 EUR"), then plan steps, then the pump list. The sheet drags to full height for filters (fuel, corridor width, reserve).
5. **Each pump row**: name, flag, price with age chip and source, "+5 km detour (est.)", and "net about 2.30 EUR vs this stretch's median". Tap = highlight on map; "Go" opens the nav-app chooser for one stop; "Route via here" opens Google Maps with up to 3 waypoints [doc design section 4].
6. **Honesty strip** under the list, always visible: route provider and whether ferries and tolls were used; "estimates, check at the pump"; countries whose prices are stale or unverified are named ("Italy: not current").
7. **Share**: button that copies a link with the plan in the URL fragment. **Clear**: removes the route and layer (exists today).
8. Mobile first: every control at least 44 px; no hover-only features; the map stays visible above the sheet (existing `setSheet('half')` behaviour).

### C4. Data needs
- Country JSON files already loaded by the app (position, price per fuel, `updated_at`, source, flags). **Add nothing server-side for v2.**
- `meta.json` for EUR conversion and the per-country source-gap and health flags (exists).
- Electricity price for PHEV: the owner's own value from the profile; Eurostat household price as a suggestion later (X7).
- Routing and geocoding through the Worker (N1). No new data files.
- Later (L1): charger tiles `chargers/{cc}.json.gz` produced by a weekly job, loaded only when the charging option is on.

### C5. Privacy (no tracking)
- No account, no analytics events about routes, no cookies added.
- Start and end points leave the device only to the geocoder and the routing proxy. The Worker rounds coordinates to about 100 m for cache keys and does **not** log them. Say so in the in-app note; update it for each provider change.
- Car profile, last route and settings stay in the browser's local storage. Share links carry the plan in the URL fragment, which browsers do not send to servers.
- "My location" is requested only when the button is pressed.
- No precise location goes to any ad vendor (ads are not part of this work).

### C6. Test plan
1. **Unit tests** (pure functions, no network): corridor distance and `along` on a hand-made polyline; detour cost; energy walk for petrol and PHEV (electric first, then petrol); saving formula incl. litres-needed; range check and reserve; greedy stop plan on a small fixed station set; staleness and unverified rules.
2. **Golden routes** (recorded route JSON saved as fixtures so tests do not call the network): Malmö to Hamburg (ferry vs bridge, 3 countries), Stockholm to Oslo, Lund to Berlin, a short single-country trip, a route with zero priced stations.
3. **Worked-example regression**: the Santa Fe numbers in section C7 must reproduce.
4. **Contract tests for the Worker** (once built): origin check rejects other sites; rounding; cache hit; provider error becomes a friendly message; no coordinates in logs.
5. **Manual device test** (owner's iPhone 13 mini, iOS 26, per the project rule that real-device recordings are ground truth): plan, share link, offline reload, "my location" denied.
6. **Data-quality test**: a route through Italy and Romania must show "not current" and must not name a stale pump "best".
7. **Performance budget**: first result under about 4 s on 4G for a 3-country route; corridor computation under ~100 ms [doc design estimates, not measured].

### C7. Worked example: owner's Hyundai Santa Fe PHEV, Malmö to Hamburg
**Car facts from the owner's pasted data (via GERMANY_SOURCE_DECISION_2026-11-06.md):** 2025 Santa Fe plug-in hybrid, petrol 1.6 L, 72 kW electric motor, 54 km WLTP electric range, 1.7 L/100 km WLTP weighted. The data lists this as "Santa Fe MX5"; I treated "MX5" as a typo and did not verify it. The car is **not** in `web/vehicles.json` today [code check], so the owner enters it by hand or we add it (small data task).

**Numbers I had to assume (the owner should replace them):**
| Item | Assumed value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol tank | 47 L | Not verified. Sets how far a full tank goes. |
| Petrol-only consumption on motorway, battery empty | 7.0 L/100 km | The 1.7 L/100 km WLTP figure is a weighted figure that assumes charging; it is not usable for range or cost on a long trip. |
| Battery energy use | 20 kWh/100 km | Not verified. |
| Real electric range on a motorway start | 40 km (54 km WLTP x ~0.75) | WLTP range is optimistic. |
| Home electricity price | 0.25 EUR/kWh | Illustrative. |
| Route | 503 km via the no-ferry car profile (measured on OSRM in ROUTE_PLANNER_DESIGN.md); 373 km via a ferry route (Valhalla, same doc) | Provider choice changes the answer. |
| Pump prices (EUR/L, 95) | SE 1.60 (Sweden published median, Oct 2026, from DATA_ACCURACY_2026-10-08.md); DK 1.85 and DE 1.80 are made up for illustration | Not live prices. |

**Case: start in Malmö with a full tank and full battery, 503 km route.**
- Electric part: first ~40 km, about `40 x 20/100 / 0.90` = 8.9 kWh, cost about 2.2 EUR at 0.25 EUR/kWh (charging efficiency 90%).
- Petrol part: 463 km x 7.0/100 = 32.4 L. Tank 47 L minus reserve 10% (4.7 L) = 42.3 L usable. **32.4 L is less than 42.3 L, so no fuel stop is needed.** Arrival fuel about 14.6 L (47 - 32.4).
- Result headline: "No fuel stop needed (about 32 L of petrol plus 9 kWh at home price)."

**Optional-stop test.** Candidate pump in Denmark, 2 km off the corridor at km 120, price 1.85 EUR/L vs the Swedish 1.60 EUR/L paid at home before leaving. A pump in Germany 1.5 km off at km 400 at 1.80 EUR/L.
- Detour km: `2 x 2 x 1.3` = 5.2 km (DK), `2 x 1.5 x 1.3` = 3.9 km (DE).
- Detour cost: battery is empty by km 40, so petrol: DK 5.2 x 7/100 x 1.85 = 0.67 EUR; DE 3.9 x 7/100 x 1.80 = 0.49 EUR.
- Both pumps are dearer than filling in Sweden at 1.60. With litres bought of 10 L, DK costs 10 x (1.85 - 1.60) + 0.67 = **+3.17 EUR worse** than not stopping. So the planner says **"no stop; fill up in Sweden before you leave"**, which matches the owner's border-run logic. (All numbers illustrative.)
- Today's planner would instead assume a full 47 L fill at each pump and rank the German pump best because it is on the route, which is wrong for this trip.

**Case where it matters: Malmö to Munich (~1,100 km, assumed).**
- Range on petrol after 40 km electric: usable 42.3 L / 7.0 x 100 = 604 km, plus 40 km electric = about 644 km. Destination is beyond that, so **one stop is needed** at about km 500 to 600, near Kassel/Würzburg.
- The planner then chooses, among dated German pumps in the corridor, the one with the best `(price - stretch median) x litres - detour`, where litres needed = `(1,100 - 600 + ...)` about 35 L plus reserve top-up. This is where the German data quality (N6, section B table) directly decides the answer; with ANWB-only prices the plan must say "German prices not verified for age".

**What this example shows for the spec:** (1) PHEV electric range matters for the first 40 km only, so the main PHEV effect on a long trip is the petrol-only consumption figure, not the 1.7 L/100 km headline; (2) the correct comparison is against the price you would pay before leaving or at home, not only against the route median; (3) most "cheapest pump" results on trips under ~600 km should be "no stop needed".

## E. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood / impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Free routing or geocoding is withdrawn or throttled. | High with the demo server; medium with a keyed free tier. | N1 Worker, caching, a second provider as a switch, friendly error text. |
| Provider terms disallow this use (e.g. non-commercial only, display-on-their-map rules). | Medium. Terms in the design doc are from docs and not all verified. | Read the terms for the chosen provider before launch; keep the owner's non-commercial stance consistent. |
| Wrong corridor because of ferries or a different road than the driver takes. | Medium. | Show the route provider and ferry flag; allow via-points (X6). |
| A "best pump" shown from a stale, unverified or mislabelled price (Germany via ANWB, Italy stale, Romania partial, SE ANWB petrol slots). | High today. | N6 gating, per-country "not current" notes, keep the 10 h cutoff, never call such a pump "best". |
| Savings overstated (compared to median, not to the driver's real alternative). | Medium. | Net-of-detour numbers, "about", reference price defined in the UI, compare to home price where known. |
| PHEV numbers misleading (WLTP figures are optimistic; weighted 1.7 L/100 km is not usable for range). | High if defaults are hidden. | Ask for petrol-only consumption, apply a real-world factor, show assumptions. |
| Privacy promise broken by a provider or the Worker logging coordinates. | Low to medium. | Round coordinates, no logging, publish the note, use URL fragments for share links. |
| Load time and memory when a route crosses 3+ countries on 4G (Germany JSON is large; estimates in the design doc are unmeasured). | Medium. | Progressive loading, keep only the needed fields, measure on the owner's device. |
