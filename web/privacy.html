About this service and contact

EuroFuelPrices is a free, non-commercial information service with no ads, no accounts and no paid features. Prices are guidance only; please check at the pump. We take no liability for the correctness of prices from third-party sources, which are named next to each price.

Contact: send a message to our Facebook Page. Security issues can also be reported privately through GitHub (see SECURITY.md).

What we store about you

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no cookies and no advertising or tracking services.

  • Visit and usage counters. A small request to our own server counts events such as "a visit", "a fuel type was chosen", "a country was viewed", "a navigation app was opened" or "the search was used". It stores only the day, the event name and a number. It never stores your IP address, your location, your search text or any identifier. The server looks at your IP address only for a moment to limit abuse, and does not keep it.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site is delivered through Cloudflare. Its cookieless analytics counts page views, countries and browsers in aggregate. Cloudflare is the operator of this service; see its privacy policy.
  • Settings in your browser. Your language, car profile, saved stations, theme and similar choices are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us and you can delete them in your browser settings at any time.

What your browser sends to other services

Some parts of the map are loaded or calculated by public services. When you use them, your browser connects to them directly, so they see your IP address and the request, as with any website:

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which serves map data from OpenStreetMap. Loaded whenever the map is shown. If the vector map cannot start, the fallback map may load tiles from Esri (server.arcgisonline.com) or the OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org) instead.
  • Address search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Only when you search for a place or enter route start and end points; the text you type is sent to find the place.
  • Route planning. Only when you plan a route; start and end coordinates go to a routing server through our own proxy. Shared plan links contain the places and settings in the link itself; nothing is stored on a server.
  • Facilities near a station. Only when you open a station; the station's position goes to OpenStreetMap Overpass servers through our proxy.
  • Navigation buttons. If you choose Google Maps, Waze, Apple Maps or HERE, the destination is passed to that app, which then follows its own rules.

We use your device location only if you ask for "near me", and only inside your browser; the position is not sent to us except as part of a nearby-facility or routing request described above.

Facebook

Our Facebook Page is run by Facebook (Meta) as well as by us. Facebook processes the data of people who visit or interact with the Page and gives us aggregate statistics. When you comment on the Page, a small program of ours may read your comment text and answer it. It keeps only the comment's technical id so that it does not answer twice, not your name or profile.

Your rights

Because we store no personal data, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold data about you anyway, contact us (above) and we will look into it. In the EU you may also complain to your data protection authority; in Sweden that is the IMY.
