Good to know


    

  • The figure is the median station price of the country, converted to euro (and shown in local currency where relevant). It is indicative, not the price at a specific pump.
    • 

  • Updated about every 15 minutes. If a country's data is unavailable the widget shows a link to the live map instead of a wrong number.
    • 

  • Prefer data? JSON and CSV of the country medians are free to reuse with a link to eurofuelprices.com. See also the European price ranking.
